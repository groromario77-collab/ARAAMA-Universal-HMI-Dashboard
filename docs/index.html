From an MQTT message to a temperature chart

Connect the tablet to your MQTT broker and let your ESP32 publish a reading. In MQTT search, select the topic and the temperature field from its JSON payload. Bind a gauge to that field and enable history to chart the readings over time.

Home Assistant and Modbus on the same panel

Import a room’s entities from Home Assistant. To add a meter, connect through Modbus TCP or an RTU-over-TCP gateway. Select the power register from the manual for your meter. HTTP/REST, WebSocket and Socket.IO sources can also supply readings.

Installation and illustrated guides

Open the APK on your Android device. Before updating an existing panel, export your project. Build 101 of version 0.1.10 can be installed over 0.1.5.
