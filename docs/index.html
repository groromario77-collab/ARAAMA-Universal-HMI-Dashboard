One Android tablet for your sensors, meters and switches

Build a dashboard that puts an ESP32 temperature sensor, a Home Assistant switch and a Modbus power meter side by side. Trace each reading to its source and see where each control sends its command.

From an MQTT message to a temperature chart

Connect the tablet to your MQTT broker and let your ESP32 publish a reading. In MQTT search, choose the topic and the temperature field from its JSON payload. Bind a gauge to that field, enable history and open a trend to see how the room changes over time.

Bring Home Assistant and Modbus onto the same panel

Import a room's entities from Home Assistant. To add a meter, connect through Modbus TCP or an RTU-over-TCP gateway and select the power register from the meter's manual. HTTP/REST, WebSocket and Socket.IO sources can also supply readings.

Installation and illustrated guides

Open the APK on your tablet. If you are updating an existing panel, export your project first. For a small MQTT setup, the 39 MiB version 0.1.5 is also available.
